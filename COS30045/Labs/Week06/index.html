About the data

The charts reconstruct the 4,233 cleaned Week 6 rows from the original television CSV already stored in Week 1. The row-selection file records which source rows appeared in the supplied Week 6 export, so I do not have to duplicate the dataset in this repository.

Figures are the values stated on energy labels. They are not household meter readings, and the dataset does not show how many TVs were sold.
