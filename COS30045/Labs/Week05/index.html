Data visualisation / D3 multi-chart webpage

Three ways to read the data.

Different questions need different visualisations. This page uses a vertical bar chart to compare categories, a line and scatter chart to show change over time, and a donut chart to show proportions.

01 / Exercise 5.1

How does energy use compare for 55-inch TVs?

I compared the average annual energy figure on the labels of similarly sized TVs, grouped by screen technology. Using one size makes the comparison more meaningful than mixing large and small screens.

Electricity prices fluctuate over time.

Data & method

One page, three purposes.

The first and third charts use summaries from the television dataset supplied for the KNIME exercises. The second chart uses the course's ARE_Spot_Prices.csv and its average-price column.

D3 loads the CSV files, converts fields to numbers, and binds the rows to SVG elements. Axes, scales, a line generator, a pie layout and an arc generator are implemented in separate scripts.
